How to play

Build, defend, conquer, and keep your lord alive
  1. Feed and house your people. Woodcutters, orchards, dairies and hunters feed them. Wheat becomes flour then bread; hops become ale. Fields freeze in winter, so fill the granary first.
  2. Arm them. Workshops fill the armory. Recruit at the barracks in the Army tab.
  3. Build a castle. Walls, towers, great towers and a gatehouse. Troops climb the walls from towers and gatehouses, and come and go through the gate. Engineers mount mangonels, ballistae and oil.
  4. Take castles. Laddermen and siege towers put troops on enemy walls. Rams break gates. Engineers fill moats. Select your army and press Storm castle: they line up just out of range of the enemy bows and engines, then charge together. Right click the castle to send them in at once.
  5. Win honor. Banquets, feasts, dances, jousts, chapels and estates. Honor raises your rank and buys estates.
  6. Keep order. Guard posts, stocks, the gaol, gallows and the rack cut crime. Fear makes people work faster but less happy.
ClickPick a building, then click the map. Drag for walls and moats. R rotates a gatehouse. DragBox-select troops. Double click a soldier to select all of that type on screen.
